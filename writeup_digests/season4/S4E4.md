### S4E4-01 · 1st · stopwhispering (Johannes Heller) · LB not stated for winner; 2nd submission 0.14372/0.14379 · CV 0.14514 (ensemble, RMSLE, 10-fold)

- **Link:** https://www.kaggle.com/c/playground-series-s4e4/discussion/499174
- **Status:** FETCHED (first `/c/` attempt returned a 331-byte shell; recovered full body + 47 comments with `X-No-Cache: true`)
- **TL;DR:** Deliberate "max out the heavy ensembling GBDT strategy" run: OpenFE features, per-family sequential feature selection, WandB Bayesian HPO, 49-model Nelder-Mead blend with negative weights, plus one external ANN submission at 17%.
- **Architecture:** FLAT · stages=1 · l1=49 own models (10-fold CV OOF each, families LGBM/XGB/CatBoost/HistGB/RF-regressor/XGB-classifier-with-regression-head/AutoGluon) + 1 external public ANN submission · l2=none (Nelder-Mead weights, no meta-learner fitted on OOF) · novel=none · mod=neg-weights,public-oof ·
  topo=OpenFE[192 generated] → per-family SFS ~20 feats → 49 models ×10 folds → Nelder-Mead weighted blend → +17% external ANN submission
  - Both ensemble steps are weight selection, not trained metas: step 1 optimizes 49 blend weights on the OOF matrix with Nelder-Mead; step 2 tunes one external submission's weight (17%) against Public LB because it has no OOF.
  - AutoGluon is only one member of the pool (CV 0.14592), so per ruling 6 this is not `AUTOML`.
  - The submitted prediction was the blend including the external ANN; his 2nd submission (same 49-model blend, no external) scored 0.14372/0.14379 and "would have finished first, too".
- **Setup:** original competition has 8 feature columns (author's comment: "For the 8 original features, it generated 192 additional features"); train/test row counts not stated.
- 10-fold CV per model; OOF predictions saved per model.
- Structural quirk exploited: use the complete original (non-synthetic) Abalone dataset for TRAINING in each fold, never for validation — "the LB dataset will have a distribution similar to the synthetic dataset" (author's intent; a commenter's reading in the discussion above).
- Submission slots: at least 2 used (winner + the no-external-blend version).
- **Features:** OpenFE (default settings, no API docs) generates ~192 candidates from the 8 originals; drop highly-correlated ones; then Sequential Feature Selection down to ca. 20 extra features, run individually per family (LGBM, XGB, CatBoost).
- Example selected features, verbatim: 'freq(Shell_weight)', '(Length-Shell_weight)', '(Whole_weight/Shucked_weight)', 'freq(Whole_weight)', '(Whole_weight+Shell_weight)', '(Length/Shell_weight)', 'residual(Whole_weight)', 'log(Whole_weight)', 'max(Whole_weight,Height)'.
- 'Sex' encoding: only OHE for models without native categorical support; every other encoding failed.
- Columns dropped: not stated (beyond the correlation-based OpenFE pruning).
- **Models:** hyperparameters found by Bayesian Optimization with WandB Sweeps for LGBM, XGB, CatBoost, HistGB, RandomForest regression.
- Best individual models, CV RMSLE (author's table): LGBM 0.14611 · CatBoost 0.14620 · XGB 0.14616 · XGB Classifier with Regression Head 0.14680 · HistGB 0.14648 · AutoGluon 0.14592.

- MSLE-metric handling: LGBM custom MSLE loss (similar to broccoli beef's proposal; 'gamma' objective only slightly worse), XGB `reg:squaredlogerror`, np.log1p/np.expm1 for the other models.
- Seeds, library versions, full hyperparameter values: not stated.
- **CV:** 10-fold CV per model; splitter named as standard folds — StratifiedKFold explicitly listed under "what did not work"; #repeats, seeds not stated.
- CV ladder: best single 0.14611 (LGBM) → ensemble 0.14514; AutoGluon solo 0.14592.

- **Ensembling:** two-step. Step 1: 49 models blended with weights optimized on the OOF matrix by Nelder-Mead; best solution used negative coefficients and weights summing to 0.997, which "consistently outperformed coefficients clipped at min=0.0 and scaled to 1.0".
- Step 2: add well-performing public notebook submissions; only one survived — an ANN-based submission by endofnight17j03 (https://www.kaggle.com/code/endofnight17j03/abalone-age-ann-xgboost-catboost) at optimal weight 17%, selected against Public LB.

- **Post-processing:** log1p/expm1 round-trip for non-native MSLE models (part of the metric handling, not a separate blend step); no threshold/rounding stated.
- **Gains:** (−0.00097 CV RMSLE) 49-model Nelder-Mead blend over the best single model: 0.14611 → 0.14514.
- (stated, unquantified) negative-coefficient weights beat clipped-to-[0,1]-and-normalized weights "consistently".
- (stated, unquantified) per-family feature selection (ca. 20 OpenFE features per family) — praised by 9th place in comments as "a simple yet brilliant idea".

## S4E4 — consensus recipe
- **Architecture distribution:** 1st `FLAT` (49-model Nelder-Mead blend + 17% external ANN) · 2nd `AUTOML` (customized AutoGluon, 5 stacking layers, is the submission) · 3rd `FLAT` (6 members: 3 AutoGluon views + 2 GBDT ensembles + public LGB) · 4th `FLAT` (AutoGluon + public LGB at fixed 50/50) · 5th `FLAT` (harmonic mean over XGB/CAT/LGB/ANN) · 8th `FLAT` (AutoGluon + public LGB) · **winner topology: `FLAT`** — five of six submit multi-model blends; the only `AUTOML` run finished 2nd, and no entrant fitted a meta-learner on OOF (no `STACK2` at all).
- **New architectures at the board:** none — every `novel=` slot in this competition is `none`; the pool is LGBM/XGB/CatBoost/HistGB/RF/AutoGluon plus vanilla ANNs. Two non-architecture curiosities: 1st's XGB *classifier with softmax regression head* (Prajwal Anagani's approach) earned a blend slot despite worse solo CV, and 1st's external ANN submission was the only directly neural member in the winning blend — though his AutoGluon member "indirectly adds some ANN to the ensemble, though" (the author's own line).
- **Agreed on (4 of 6 — S4E4-01, -02, -03, -04):** OpenFE as the feature generator (1st: default settings, 8→192; 2nd: whitelisted generators + custom quantile generators, ~200; 3rd: customized for RMSLE; 4th: named as step one, no selection described).
- **Agreed on (3 of 6 — S4E4-01, -02, -03):** hard pruning/selection of the OpenFE output before training (1st: SFS → ~20 feats per family; 2nd: AutoGluon FeatureSelector with LightGBM proxy, 1 h; 3rd: RFE+LogReg → 30 feats, fold-averaged). 4th states OpenFE only.
- **Agreed on (3 of 6 — S4E4-01, -03, -04):** explicit MSLE/RMSLE metric handling inside training (1st: custom MSLE loss for LGBM, `reg:squaredlogerror` for XGB, log1p/expm1 for the rest; 3rd: OpenFE customized for RMSLE; 4th: log-transformed target + custom RMSLE metric in AutoGluon).
- **Agreed on (3 of 6 — S4E4-03, -04, -08):** trupologhelper's public `ps4e4-lightgbm-only` notebook as an ensemble member — the same artifact appears at 3rd, 4th and 8th (4th blends it at 50%; 8th's blend weights unstated).
