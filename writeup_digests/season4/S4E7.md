  meta-learned by a DenseLight NN (LightGBM gave a near-identical result), fed by denoising-autoencoder latents.
- **Architecture:** STACK2 · stages=2 · l1=38 models (>=8 CatBoost, then "6-8 each of" xLearn FM, xLearn FFM, Keras FM and

  - The submitted prediction is the stack, not the strong CatBoost single (whose numbers are the only ones in her table).
  - Ruling 6: the AutoGluon models are just two more level-1 members, so the tag is not `AUTOML`.
  - Ruling 5 applied to the DAE: the autoencoder latents enter level-1 models as FEATURES, so `mod=embed` — they are not a level.

  - Gender 2, Age 66, Driving_License 2, Region_Code 54, Previously_Insured 2, Vehicle_Age 3, Vehicle_Damage 2,
    Annual_Premium 55068, Policy_Sales_Channel 156, Vintage 290
- Denoising autoencoder (DAE) latents: `pd.get_dummies` on the categorical data; `['Age', 'Annual_Premium', 'Vintage']` kept
  numeric and scaled to 0-1 instead of dummied (otherwise the matrix is too wide); bottleneck of 3 and 8 factors, 8 better.
  - DAE factors are weak alone, best when ADDED to the other features; they lifted individual CatBoost and Keras embedding
    models by about +0.0002.
  - They also killed the FM family: after adding latents, "there were millions of unique values and couldn't be converted to categories".
- Feature sets from a public discussion (516475) used by some models; some CatBoost models with and without DAE features.
- Dropped columns: none stated; `max_bins`-style reductions not used (see Failed).
- **Models:** best 5 individual model types by CV, published table:
