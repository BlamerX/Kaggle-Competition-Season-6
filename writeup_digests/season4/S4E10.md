### S4E10-01 · 1st · Hardy Xu (hardyxu52) · LB 0.97344/0.96938 · CV 0.97059

- **TL;DR:** A three-level stack: three Optuna-tuned GBM families plus a paddykb-inspired NN, each refined by a CatBoost-on-logit-baseline,
  then an NN that stacks the four refined predictions.
- The CatBoost-baseline "secret sauce" lifted even the CatBoost member's own prediction enough to have taken 3rd on its own.
- **Architecture:** STACKN · stages=3 · l1=4 family predictions (XGBoost / LightGBM / CatBoost, each the average of 10 Optuna configs,
  plus one NN inspired by paddykb) ·
  l2=4 CatBoost residual-baseline refinements (each family's prediction fed back as an init-score/logit baseline) ·
  l3=NN stacker over the 4 refined predictions · novel=none · mod=cascade (the level-1→2 refine is residual boosting, not a blender) ·
  topo=3 Optuna-10-avg GBM families + NN -> per-family [CatBoost trained on that family's OOF logits as baseline] -> NN stack over the 4 refined -> submission
  - Genuine multi-stage: level-0 predictions become the BASELINE (raw logits) for a second CatBoost at level-1 (residual boosting), then an
    NN meta at level-2 stacks the four - the submitted prediction is the NN stack, so this is neither flat nor a single stack.
  - The cascade stage is what is unusual: the author states CatBoost "significantly improved the performance of each of these model
    predictions, even the ones originally generated using CatBoost."
  - No public notebooks - he trains locally; the winning model is the only artifact, no shared OOF pool.
- **Setup:** Binary classification, ROC AUC. Data split 60-40 train/test, public LB = 20% of test = 8% of the full set; CV therefore measures
  60% of the data and is the reliable signal. Included the original dataset. #rows/#cols not stated. Submission slots not stated.
- **Features:** Treat numerical features as categorical AND keep the numeric feature plus a categorical copy of it. No other feature
  engineering ("feature engineering is of little use" in playgrounds). Included the original dataset. Tip credit: large `max_bin` values
  (from @omidbaghchehsaraei's post). Encodings, GP features, dropped columns beyond the numeric+categorical-copy: not stated.
- **Models:** Per family XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost: Optuna finds 10 optimal hyperparameter sets, predictions averaged = one family output.
  A Neural Network heavily inspired by @paddykb's `ps-s4e10-no-keras-no-loan-cv-0-963` notebook. Then a second CatBoost per family trained
  on the family's OOF logits used as `baseline` (init-score). Base-model family scores (CV / pub / priv): LGBM .96811/.97005/.96637,
  XGB .96767/.96989/.96540, CatBoost .96972/.97299/.96865, NN .96678/.97088/.96577. Final-stacked scores: CV .97059 / pub .97344 / priv .96938.
  Exact Optuna search spaces, seeds, fold counts, NN architecture: not stated.
- **CV:** Splitter not named; OOF predictions (not a fold split) used for the baselines (author comment: "for the baseline I did use oof
  predictions"); fold count not stated on this page; #repeats not stated.
- CV-vs-LB for the winner: CV .97059 -> public .97344 (+0.00285) -> private .96938 (-0.00121 below CV); the public->private shakeup is large.
- Best single refined member (CatBoost-on-CatBoost baseline) was CV .96997 / private .96903, "enough for 3rd place" on its own.
- **Ensembling:** Level-1 = 10 Optuna configs averaged within each of the three GBM families (the NN is a single paddykb-inspired model).
  Level-2 = a CatBoost residual-baseline per family (baseline = that
  family's OOF probabilities converted to logits, since no library natively returns logits). Level-3 = a Neural Network that stacks the four
  refined predictions. Shared-OOF: none (all self-generated, local training).
- **Post-processing:** For the CatBoost baseline he converts predicted probabilities to logits ("raw values") because a binary-classification
  baseline needs logits; the submitted value is the NN stacker's probability (AUC is rank-based, no threshold applied).

- (largest structural result) the CatBoost-baseline residual step improved EVERY family, including the family that was already a CatBoost.

## S4E10 — consensus recipe
- **Architecture distribution:** 1st `STACKN` (Optuna families -> CatBoost logit-baseline cascade -> NN stack) · 2nd `FLAT` (hill-climbing weighted
