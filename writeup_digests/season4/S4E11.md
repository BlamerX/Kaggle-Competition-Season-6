- **Comments:** technical content in author replies and commenters (mined above):
  - Why AUC over ACC: ACC "discards information first by converting all prob predictions into integers 0/1 with `p=(pred>0.5)`"; AUC keeps the
    full float probability, so it is more informative; "ACC not smooth" = hill climbing stops too early and the LB score is worse (links to the
    s4e11 metric discussions 543929 and 543685).
  - giim adds ACC is a low-resolution metric invariant under many transforms (moving a class-1 score 0.8->0.9 leaves ACC identical but AUC improves).

## S4E11 — consensus recipe
- **Architecture distribution:** 1st `AUTOML` (24 models, AutoGluon blender) · 4th `AUTOML` (AutoGluon + LightAutoML equal-weight blend) ·
  13th `AUTOML` (79 OOFs, AutoGluon blender; Ridge/Lasso/hill-climb as alternates) · 25th `FLAT` (33/33/33 CatBoost+XGBoost+MLP).
