## S6E5 — consensus recipe
- **Architecture distribution:** 1st `STACK2` (LR-on-logits + AutoGluon metas over 186 OOFs incl 4 L2, blended 50-50) · 2nd `STACK2` (cuML LR on logits of 218 level-1 models) ·
  4th `FLAT` (hill climbing 265→57, blended with a peer ensemble) · 5th `STACK2` (sklearn LR on 99 base logits; rank-avg with HC left unsubmitted) ·
  7th `STACKN` (AutoGluon stack-of-stackers over 176 models, 3-4 levels + pseudo-labeling) · 8th `STACKN` (explicit L1→L5, self-distilled L4, averaged over 5/7/10-fold splits) ·
  10th `STACKN` (stack of stacks → cuML LR, best CV 221 but submitted 129) · 11th `STACKN` (level-2/level-3 stacking, GPU HC + Ridge, 250 OOFs) · 17th `STACK2` (800-feature store → 130 singles → 2 AutoGluon stackings → HC/LR/Ridge).
  - **winner topology: `STACK2`** — the top 3 (1st/2nd/5th) all submitted a single Logistic-Regression-on-logits meta over ~100-218 diverse level-1 OOFs (2nd literally "no stacking"; 1st added only 4 nested L2 columns). The four explicitly DEEP stacks — 7th (stack-of-stackers), 8th (L5), 10th (stack-of-stacks), 11th (L2/L3) — finished 7th/8th/10th/11th, behind the level-2 stacks. Multi-stage depth was tried hardest here and did NOT win: extra levels beyond 2 were neutral-to-harmful once the pool is already diverse.
- **New architectures at the board:** `RealMLP` is the undisputed strongest base model — 2nd's best single family (CV 0.954426, 40 models), 5th's MLP family matching the GBT ceiling (0.950-0.954), and named "strongest single model" by 7th and "most important family" by 8th (via @yekenot's notebook).
  - `Fine-tuned TabICL`: 2nd's 6th family (CV 0.950827) and, per @cdeotte's comment on 8th's writeup, the 2nd-largest absolute weight in his 218-model LR meta.
  - `TabM` broadly present (2nd 0.953371, 8th orig-row 0.9526, 11th in the final blend, 17th as a diversity filler beating GBDTs in 1-2 folds).
  - Diversity-only exotic architectures that scored LOW solo but were kept: Deep FFM / xLearn FFM (5th's most-diverse, 0.852/0.948, the single most valuable decorrelator), BART (5th, 0.851 Spearman), Nyström kernel, GraphSAGE/GNN, Trompt, ModernNCA, LNN (2nd's 0.893791 accidental inclusion), TabR/RealTabR, TabPFN (8th 0.9488, 10th), TabNet (11th stuck ~0.93, rejected), DCN/NODE/GANDALF/AutoInt/SAINT/GrowNet/ExcelFormer/AMFormer (2nd's long tail).
  - Novel-ish non-transformer picks: LogisticGAM (11th — beat Logistic Regression on 50 stochastic 10k samples), cuML Random Forest and Yggdrasil (17th, diversity only), RGF (8th), EBM/NGBoost (5th).
  - No entrant used a novel architecture as the META learner: every level-2 top of the podium is logistic regression (cuML LR for 2nd/10th, sklearn LR for 5th, LR-with-Logits for 1st).
- **Agreed on (8 of 9) — the winning meta:** a LINEAR logistic-regression-on-LOGITS blender over a wide diverse OOF pool, not a tree blender and not a deep stack.
  - 2nd: cuML LR on logits, add-everything-above-0.930. 5th: sklearn LR, C=1.0 L2, class_weight=None, logits clipped ±30, 5-seed averaged. 1st: LR-with-Logits + AutoGluon metas. 10th: cuML LR as the consistent top stacker (beat CatBoost/Ridge/HC). 11th: Ridge over HC/LR (LR stacker lost to Ridge). 7th/8th/17th: LR/logistic present at the top layer alongside HC/AutoGluon.
  - Corollary from 2nd (with data): model/feature selection before the LR meta measurably hurt vs feeding LR everything.
- **Agreed on (6 of 9) — the single reliable feature win:** append the ORIGINAL F1-strategy dataset as extra training ROWS, and separately exploit it as column-level features; the two usages carry different signal.
  - 5th: "the one robustly positive signal" / "the reliable win". 8th: orig-row vs orig-col blend was his whole diversity axis. 17th: "original data as rows" + TE of original columns. 1st: original-data sample weights 0.5-1 optimal, 0.25 hurt. 2nd: original.csv had genuine signal (XGB needed max_depth ~6-8). 4th: relies on @yekenot's feature set built on the original data.
- **Agreed on (2 of 9):** drop the `Driver` column in at least some models — @ravi20076's per-variable adversarial AUC flagged Driver as the most distribution-shifted variable. 1st built "Driver_Dropped" models ("boosted the score a little"); 11th retrained without Driver (small gain). 7th and 17th do the opposite (both count-encoded Driver), so this is a 2-of-9 practice, not a consensus.
- **Agreed on (5 of 9) — the standard FE primitives:** target encoding (fold-safe / year-aware / Race×Compound / pair), frequency/count encoding, digit-level & rounded transforms, n-gram & pair target-encoder interactions, category twins of numeric columns.
- **Agreed on (4 of 9) — the only published fold scheme:** fixed `random_state=42` stratified folds — 17th `StratifiedKFold(5, rs=42, shuffle=True)` double-stratified by year+target; 11th 5-fold target encoding; 2nd local 5-fold everywhere (public 10/15-fold notebooks rerun at 5); 5th fold-wise meta fit; 8th deliberately varies folds (5/7/10) as a diversity axis. 1st/4th/7th/10th do not state fold counts.
- **Divergences:** top-3 converged on level-2 LR-logit stacks with ~100-218 members and finished 0.00001 apart on private (1st 0.95503 / 2nd 0.95502); 3rd place's score is not published anywhere in the set, and 5th's unsubmitted rank-average (0.95505) would have topped the board. The mid-board went structurally deeper (7th stack-of-stackers, 8th L5, 10th stack-of-stacks, 11th L2/L3) and finished 7th-11th — of those four only 8th published a private score (0.95487, 0.00016 behind the winner); 7th gives only a ±0.1 bps submission delta and 10th/11th publish no LB scores, so added depth demonstrably neither recovered nor beat the level-2 stacks.
  - 10th self-diagnosed the depth penalty: the 221-model heavily-stacked AutoGluon pool had the best CV (0.955630) yet the leaner 129-model stack (0.955294) generalized better on private, with the LR spending effort on negative weights (-0.535 on an AutoGluon L3 bag) to undo redundancy.
  - Divergence on public-LB trust: 5th, 10th and 8th all say public LB/OOF CV disagreed with private; 10th's unselected submission would have been ~5th, 5th's unsubmitted rank-average would have topped private, 2nd's discarded morning pick was 0.95506.
- **Highest-leverage single trick:** rank-average two blends that sit on different probability scales instead of averaging probabilities — 5th's one-liner `rank_avg = 0.5*(rankdata(hc)/len(hc) + rankdata(ls)/len(ls))` beat the raw-probability average by +0.00002 private (0.95505 vs 0.95503) and, being AUC-only, costs nothing.
  - Runner-up (base-model side): RealMLP on the original-row feature set as the single strongest model family (2nd 0.954426, 8th 0.9545, best single across the board).

- **Nothing worked:** generator-fingerprint FE specific to Kaggle synthetics — digit features, snap-to-grid, anchor TE, TF-IDF — had "no meaningful impact here" (5th), even though they were the known artifact-FE playbook (2nd confirms artifact FE was already fully discovered).
- **Nothing worked:** stacking depth beyond level 2 as a win condition — every L3+/L5 entry (7th/8th/10th/11th) finished behind the three level-2 LR stacks; 10th explicitly crossed diminishing returns into large negative meta weights.
- **Nothing worked:** naive high-level stacking without conservatism — 8th found LR/higher stacks "easily overfit when many correlated OOFs are used" and had to fall back to averaged conservative blends; 5th found the greedy HC subset had a wider CV→public gap than his flat L2 stack.
- **Nothing worked:** near-duplicate / transductive add-ons on an already-saturated AUC — 5th's kNN label transfer (~99% accurate on the closest 1% of test rows but OOF gate never cleared) and a non-linear residual LightGBM on the stack logits (no honest OOF gain).
- **Nothing worked:** a few individual architectures — TabNet stuck ~0.93 (11th), FT-Transformer/Linear/GNN/Yggdrasil/cuML-RF "way below GBDT" kept only as diversity (17th), and the 0.893791 LNN that 2nd admitted including by mistake.
- **Nothing worked:** blind leaderboard blending as a strategy (11th avoided it, 10th refused it, 5th paid for over-conservatism instead) — though 17th's final submission-level LB-max blend is the one place it was leaned on.
