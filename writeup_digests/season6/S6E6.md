### S6E6-08 · 8th · jerry34 · LB not stated/not stated · CV not stated

- **Link:** https://www.kaggle.com/c/playground-series-s6e6/writeups/8th-place-solution
- **Status:** FETCHED (single attempt)
- **TL;DR:** Replaced balanced accuracy with class-weighted log loss as the CV metric (with a full proof it shares BA's optimal decision) and stacked 27 stackers + 60 bases under one logistic regression.
- Publishes no score at all; the entry's value is the surrogate-loss math and the prior-correction recipe.
- **Architecture:** STACKN · stages=3 · l1=60 base models · l2=27 stacker models trained over the bases · l3=Logistic Regression on stackers+bases ·
  novel=RealMLP,TabPFN (as level-1 members) · mod=public-oof ·
  topo=60 bases → 27 stackers → final LogReg over [27 stacker cols + 60 base cols] → submitted
  - The submitted prediction is the level-3 LogReg; base models feed it directly AND through the stacker layer (hence 3 levels, not a plain stack).
  - Every level-1 member is first normalized onto the same tilted-posterior scale `eta_c/pi_c` before entering the stacker — that is the architectural twist.
- **Setup:** 3 classes; priors `[0.653818, 0.202899, 0.143283]` (published verbatim); rows/cols, fold count, slots: not stated.
- Structural quirk exploited: BA = accuracy on the class-balanced reweighting, so the metric's Bayes rule is a fixed prior division, not a tuned threshold.
- **Features:** not stated (all FE lives inside the 60 base models / public notebooks).
- **Models:** level-1 zoo stated only by constraint: GBMs and logistic regression trained with `1/pi_c`-weighted losses (Route A); RealMLP and TabPFN cannot take sample weights, so unweighted + post-hoc divide-by-prior + renormalize (Route B).
- Hyperparameters: not stated.
- **CV:** validation metric = negative class-weighted log loss instead of BA, weighted `w(c) = 1/(K*pi_c)` — chosen because BA is piecewise-constant, improper and high-variance ("flipping a handful of near-tie rows moves it. That is exactly the leaderboard noise we saw").
- Numerical CV/LB values: none published.
- Trust verdict: ignored public LB entirely after checking that constant winners (cdeotte, optimistix, milanfx) sat far below public leaders → "top 100 on public leaderboard are all overfitting."
- Author's body caveat on the proof: "discussed with Fable", "generated by Opus 4.8", and "I am not 100% sure whether it's correct."
- **Ensembling:** final = logistic regression over 27 stacker columns + 60 base columns, built on cdeotte's GPU stacker notebook pattern.
- Hill climbing in logit space was tried as the blender and was worse than the LogReg.
- **Post-processing:** the prior division `q_c ∝ eta_c/pi_c` (Route B) is the post-processing; argmax taken once at the end.
- **Gains:** (unquantified, core design) weighted-log-loss CV removes the BA row-flip noise that made public-driven selection impossible.
- (unquantified) unified probability scale across weighted and unweighted members before stacking.
- **Failed:** hill-climbing blend in logit space — "not as good as logistic regression."
- **Comments:** cdeotte replies that the weighted-log-loss insight "is great advice because balanced accuracy is noisy. I will try this in future competitions including July playground."
- Georgy Mamarin: names it the standard others skip — validate on a strictly-proper surrogate sharing BA's optimum; he is folding the recipe into his notebook-standards doc, credited to jerry34.
- **Compute:** not stated.
- **Artifacts:** no notebook URLs cited in body (Chris's GPU stacker named without link)
