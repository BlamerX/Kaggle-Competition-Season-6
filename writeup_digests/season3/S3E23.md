## S3E23 — consensus recipe
- **Architecture distribution:** 2nd `FLAT` (8-member hill-climb + two re-blends; no meta-learner fitted on OOF).
  Winner topology for this file: `FLAT` - one entry only, so the tag stands on its own evidence: the author names
  hill-climbing explicitly and every published step is a weighted combination.
- **New architectures at the board:** none. Members are sklearn/scikit-GBM stock: RF, Extra Trees, HGB, LGBM,
  XGB, CatBoost, Nyström-approximated LogisticRegression, plain ANN. The Nyström LogReg is the oldest trick in the
  book, not a novel tabular architecture; it nonetheless delivered this file's largest measured blend gain (+0.00029 LB).
- **What this one solution demonstrates (no cross-entry agreement exists to count):**
  - Log-transform of all inputs, imported from the community (ambrosm), as the only feature change worth keeping.
  - Diversity-first flat ensembling with hill-climbing weights, blended in stages: same-family GBMs first
    (0.7907), then a linear-kernel outlier (+0.00029), then a NN (+0.00002).
  - Dimensionality reduction is NOT a substitute for the transform: PCA at >99% explained variance still lost to
    log-transformed raw features.
- **Divergences:** unobservable within this file (single entry). The one internal split: his CV said 0.793 on an
  untransformed baseline while every published improvement is an LB step - this file's 2nd-place entry steered the
  last two blend decisions on Public LB, not CV.
- **Highest-leverage single trick:** add one structurally-different member (Nyström LogisticRegression) to an
  already-hill-climbed GBM blend: +0.00029 LB, 15x the NN step that followed - S3E23-02.
- **Nothing worked:** PCA instead of log-transform, t-SNE class separation, clustering, and target encoding on
  clusters - all four tried and reported as no-gain by the 2nd placer himself.
