## S3E11 — consensus recipe
- **Architecture distribution:** 1st `STACK2` (18-model zoo → ridge weights) · 4th `FLAT` (5 GBMs,
  Optuna weights, stacking explicitly rejected) · 7th `STACK2` (~20 multi-view GBM/GAM bases → ridge
  meta) · 17th `FLAT` (3 blind team approaches, XGB/CAT/LGBM).
  - **winner topology: `STACK2`** — ridge on the L1 predictions; and 7th used the same meta family
    (ridge), while the 4th in between finished higher than 7th with a plain Optuna-weighted blend,
    so the board does not rank stacks over flats — it ranks FE over both.
- **New architectures at the board:** none — every submitted member is GBM/RF/ET/NN/GAM/Ridge,
  `novel=none` at all four ranks (S3E11-01, -04, -07, -17). Closest miss: `TabNet` tried and dropped
  by 7th; one `Keras` neural net survived as a single L1 member in 1st's zoo (labeled "Keras" in his
  chart; not novel-class).
- **Agreed on (3 of 4 — S3E11-01, S3E11-04, S3E11-07):** log-target regression — model `log1p(cost)`
  / `np.log(cost)` and invert predictions; 1st uses log1p+expm1 for every model and blends in log
  space, 7th log1p+expm1, 4th np.log with rmse objective. (S3E11-17 silent.)
- **Agreed on (3 of 4 — S3E11-01, S3E11-04, S3E11-07):** add the original (non-synthetic) data to
  training — "the score improves" (1st), fold -1 trick training-only (4th), "improved my CV score
  and my public leaderboard position quite well" (7th). S3E11-17 only implies it via
  `avg_df_original`, so it is not counted.
- **Agreed on (4 of 4):** aggressive column reduction — nobody trains on the full schema:
  1st blends over competing subsets, 4th keeps 9 columns, 7th keeps 4-6 per model, 17th keeps 8.
- **Agreed on (3 of 4 — S3E11-01, S3E11-07, S3E11-17):** the duplicate/quasi-duplicate structure is
