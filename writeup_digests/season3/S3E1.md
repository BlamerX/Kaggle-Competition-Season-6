## S3E1 — consensus recipe
- **Architecture distribution:** 1st `AUTOML` (AutoGluon TabularPredictor, 3-level internal stack, vendor-managed submission) · 2nd `FLAT` (blend of public boosting + own models + one keras_tuner NN) · 24th `FLAT` (XGB+LGB+CB 10-fold ensemble).
  - **winner topology: `AUTOML`** — the top of the board delegated the entire ensemble to a framework, while 2nd and 24th hand-built flat blends.
  - Read-across: no entrant published a trained meta-learner; nothing here is `STACK2`/`STACKN` except the AutoGluon stack the 1st never tuned himself.
- **New architectures at the board:** none — every model across all three entries is a stock 2023 choice (XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, Random Forest, Keras MLP, or the AutoGluon wrapper). No FT-Transformer/TabM/NODE-type architecture appears.
- **Agreed on (3 of 3 — S3E1-01, S3E1-02, S3E1-24): coordinate/distance features are the core primitive.** 1st uses dmitryuarov's coordinate notebook verbatim; 2nd states "distance using coordinates plays a big part"; 24th builds distance-to-city, distance-to-coastline, PCA/rotated/polar coordinates.
- **Agreed on (2 of 3 — S3E1-02, S3E1-24): hand-built blends of the standard GBM trio (XGBoost/LightGBM/CatBoost).** 1st runs the same families only as AutoGluon level-0 members, not a hand-selected blend.
- **Agreed on (2 of 3 — S3E1-02, S3E1-24): keep the original/external California data OUT of your validation rows.** 2nd splits on the original data and adds external data only after; 24th excludes the "original" dataset from the 80/20 CV "to get more accurate scores"; 1st stayed silent on augmentation (deferred to links).
- **Divergences:** 1st trusted an AutoML framework to own the entire stack (CV 0.5006) while 24th trusted a hand-tuned 3-GBM blend and only Optuna-tuned XGBoost — the winning margin was framework breadth + community FE, not bespoke modeling.
- **Highest-leverage single trick:** geometry-aware FE — distances from lat/long to cities and coastline, plus rotated/polar/PCA coordinate encodings — cited by all three (S3E1-24 lists the exact set; S3E1-01 links the notebook).
- **Nothing worked:** (author-reported) adding external data before splitting (misaligned CV, per S3E1-02's diagnosis of *other* notebooks) and blindly chasing the public LB (S3E1-24 judged the top public entries "slightly overfit"). No entrant published a quantified failed-model or failed-feature ablation — dead-ends here are anti-knowledge by omission, not by measurement.
