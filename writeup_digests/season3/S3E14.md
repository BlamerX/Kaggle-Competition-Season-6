## S3E14 — consensus recipe
- **Architecture distribution:** 1st `STACK2` (LAD meta over 8 public OOF sets + decisive deterministic
  clone-value post-processing) · 4th `FLAT` (hill-climb over 5 public notebooks, nothing fitted) ·
  7th `STACK2` (LAD meta over 4 own families + public notebooks).
  winner topology: `STACK2` — but the winner's margin came from the Post-processing layer, which
  Ruling 2 keeps out of the stage count.
- **New architectures at the board:** none submitted — the `novel` slot is `none` on all three entries
  (stock LAD / GBM / linear pool). `TabNet` appeared only as a failure (7th). FLAML and PCA/PLS are
  tooling/features, not novel architectures; no FLAML artifact was itself the submitted stack (Ruling 6
  checked).
- **Agreed on (3 of 3 — S3E14-01, S3E14-04, S3E14-07):** the community post-processing trick
  (discussion 407327): 1st automated it into a tiered origin-value waterfall, 4th applied it inside CV
  splits and after blending, 7th applied one rounding pass after ensembling. This is the single
  shared, rank-moving practice of the competition.
- **Agreed on (3 of 3 — S3E14-01, S3E14-04, S3E14-07):** building directly on public notebooks — 1st's
  entire level-1 is 8 community OOF sets; 4th's entire pool is community notebooks (hill-climb over 5
  blended public prediction sets — 6 notebooks credited in the body; zero own training); 7th blended
  top public notebooks into his own LAD stack.
- **Agreed on (2 of 3 — S3E14-01, S3E14-07):** an LADRegression fitted on the OOF matrix as the
  blender (7th: chosen over ridge and optuna weighting). 4th used hill-climb weight selection instead —
  Ruling 1 keeps his combiner `FLAT`, not a stack.
- **Fitted vs weight-selected (the S3E14 discriminator):** fitted metas appear in 1st and 7th (LAD);
  pure weight selection in 4th (greedy hill climb); 7th's small manual public-LB reweighting after the
  LAD fit is the only public-LB-driven weight change any entry confesses to — and he regrets it.
- **Divergences:** all three rode the same public notebooks, so the rank spread came from the layers
  around the blend: 1st's integer-scale clone-value corrections > 7th's single rounding pass and
  original-data-in-train-folds CV scheme > 4th's blender-only innovation. No numeric MAE deltas are
  computable — none of the three publishes a ranked-submission score; 7th's title is the only pub/priv
  pair (3rd public vs 7th private, a shakeup in the wrong direction after public-LB weight fiddling).
- **Highest-leverage single trick:** the `fruitset`/`fruitmass` → exact-origin-`yield` assignment
  (S3E14-01; started with the manual 1945.53061 case, fruitset 0.335339 / fruitmass 0.233554) — worth
  "an integer sometimes" in MAE where every blend step bought floating points.
- **Nothing worked:** exotic/diverse model hunting by 7th — TabNet, NNs, linear models, GAM,
  ExtraTrees, XGBoost with absolute-error objective all failed (adaubas independently confirmed the
