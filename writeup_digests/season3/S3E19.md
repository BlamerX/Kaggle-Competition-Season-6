### S3E19-03 · 3rd · albertzhang314159 (Albert Zhang) · LB not stated/not stated · CV 4.87 (validation SMAPE)

- **Link:** https://www.kaggle.com/c/playground-series-s3e19/discussion/428682
- **Status:** FETCHED (`/c/` form first try, 10641 bytes, body + comments). The `/competitions/writeups/` retry returned a
  200-byte shell, so the comment count is from the `/c/` render plus a browser check: heading reads `2 Comments`.
- **TL;DR:** EDA-first: correct the 2020 anomaly with STL, divide every series by hand-derived multiplicative fractions,
  then train ONE LightGBM per product. Explicitly no ensemble.
- **Architecture:** SINGLE · stages=1 · l1=5 (one LightGBM per product; store-level series summed away, countries pooled
  into the same model) · l2=none (no meta-learner; author: "I didn't do any ensmeble") · novel=none · mod=none ·
  topo=STL trend correction of 2020 -> divide N by F_p F_c F_s B_y (standardize) -> 5 per-product LightGBM(linear_tree, dart)
  on Fourier + LMM-target-encoded date/holiday features -> multiply prediction back by (S+1) B_y F_p F_c F_s -> per-store split by F_s
  - PER-PRODUCT (5 models), GLOBAL over countries and stores: this is the board's only explicit statement of the
    global-vs-per-series choice ("Since seasonalities are different among products, I trained each product separately.
    So there are 5 models"), and the reason: product seasonality differs, store behaviour does not.
  - DIRECT multi-step, not recursive: he removes lags on purpose - "Since private score is based on the last 75% of 2022,
    lag features are not needed" - so every 2022 day is predicted independently from calendar features.
  - The multiplicative reconstruction back to `num_sold` is arithmetic on model output plus hand-estimated fractions, so
    it is not a second training stage; stages stays 1. The submitted model is a fitted estimator (LightGBM trees plus the
    linear-mixed-model encodings), so this entry is not the rule-based case of tag ruling 11.
  - Not `FLAT` and not `STACK2`: no blending at all - the 5 models predict disjoint product subsets, they are never averaged.
- **Setup:** 75 categories (products x countries x stores implied); private LB is "the last 75% of 2022" (his statement);
  training data split by year into 5 folds; prediction target is the standardized residual-like quantity `S`, not raw sales.
- Structural quirk exploited: the 2020 sales trend is abnormal, and "for unknown reason all countries in 2022 have the
  same fraction, i.e., 0.2. Probably due to data construction error."
- **Features:** baseline decomposition of each category's level: `B_{y,p,c,s} = B_y * F_{y,p} * F_{y,c} * F_{y,s}`
  (B = baseline level, F = fraction, y = year, p = product, c = country, s = store).
- Trend correction, 5 steps: (1) daily fraction of each of the 75 categories; (2) STL decomposition to extract the trend
  from daily total sales; (3) replace the trend from 2020-02-15 to 2020-11-15 with the average trend of the other years;
  (4) restore daily total sales; (5) restore each category by its fraction.
- Country fraction: `F_{y,c} = f_{y,c}` for y = 2017..2021; `F_{2022,c} = 0.2` for every country (empirical fractions are
  correlated with GDP per capita, but GDP is not used as an input).
- Product fraction, constant across years as a weighted average:
  `F_{2022,p} = F_{y,p} = (2*(f_{2017,p} + f_{2019,p} + f_{2021,p}) + 3*(f_{2018,p} + f_{2020,p})) / 12`
  (odd years weight 2, even years weight 3, because of the visible two-year seasonality).
- Store fraction: `F_{2022,s} = F_{y,s} = f_s` (fluctuation "negligible"; all stores behave the same).
- Baseline of total daily sales: `B_y = median_y`; `B_2022 ~ 18900` left unknown and set by LB probing.
- Standardization: `S_{y,p,c,s,d} = N_{y,p,c,s,d} / F_{y,p} / F_{y,c} / F_{y,s} / B_y - 1` (N = num_sold).
- Store summation (training/validation target): `S_{y,p,c,d} = N_{y,p,c,d} / F_{y,p} / F_{y,c} / B_y - 1`.
- Back-transform for prediction: `N_{y,p,c,s,d} = (S_{y,p,c,d} + 1) * B_y * F_{y,p} * F_{y,c} * F_{y,s}`.
- Temporal embedding: Fourier terms up to order 3 for week seasonality; Fourier terms up to order 5 for two-year seasonality.
- Two categorical features, target-encoded to numeric levels: `c_holiday` = country + corresponding holidays;
  `c_year_date` = country + the 365 dates of a year.
- Encoding method: linear mixed model `S ~ temporal variables` grouped by the categorical variable, computed separately for
  each product, with 4~5 fold out-of-sample predictions to avoid overfitting.
- No lag features (deliberate). No feature selection performed: "I didn't do feature selection".
- All model parameters were put into an attached spreadsheet (`baseline_parameters.xlsx`, link in Artifacts).
- **Models:** LightGBM (`lightbm` in his text) with linear tree and dart; `num_leaves` deliberately small (~5);
  `num_iterations` chosen by cross-validation RMSE; the final model is refit on the whole training set with that optimal
  `num_iterations`. Five instances, one per product. All other hyperparameters, seeds, library version: not stated.
- **CV:** training data split by year into 5 folds, then cross-validated; early-stopping/size chosen on CV **RMSE** while
  the reported local metric is SMAPE.
- Validation SMAPE: 4.87. CV-vs-LB gap: not computable (he publishes no LB score).
- Trust verdict: he trusts the year-fold CV for model sizing, but hands the single most important future-year constant
  (`B_2022`) to the LB.
- **Ensembling:** none - "I didn't do any ensmeble." Number of models averaged: 0 (the 5 models are disjoint per-product fits).
- Shared-OOF usage: none; he credits @paddykb, @kdmitrie and "all other people who contributed ideas" in discussion, not predictions.
- **Post-processing:** two deterministic steps:
- `B_2022` (the 2022 baseline level, ~18900) determined by LB probing: guess a few values, fit a parabola to the scores,
  try the minimum, add the result and fit again, repeat 1-2 times.
- The `(S+1) * B_y * F_p * F_c * F_s` reconstruction and the store split by `F_s` (arithmetic, per ruling 2 not a stage).
- **Gains:** only one number is published: validation SMAPE 4.87 for the finished model; no A-vs-B deltas are given.
- Qualitative ordering he measured: 2020 trend correction (steps 1-5) before anything else; store summation justified by
  small-integer series such as "Using LLMs to Win Friends and Influence People - Argentina - Kaggle Learn" that
  "oscillates between several integers" and "may affect model training".
- **Failed:** no lag features - deliberately dropped, not tried and rejected (he reasons the private window is contiguous
  with the training window, so lags are unnecessary).
- GDP: measured as correlated with the country fraction, then not used (the 2022 anomaly made it useless).
- Two-year product seasonality: he refused to model it as a constant fraction and pushed it into the Fourier embedding
