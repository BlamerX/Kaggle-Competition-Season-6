## S3E17 — consensus recipe
- **Architecture distribution:** 3rd `STACK2` (own meta model over 5 vendor-AutoML stacks + 2 public submissions) ·
  11th `STACK2` (LogReg meta-classifier over 4 winning classic models, neg weights allowed) ·
  17th `SINGLE` (one Optuna-tuned CPU CatBoost) · 47th `SINGLE` (CatBoostClassifier under repeated K-Fold).
  - **winner topology: `STACK2`** — but its level-1 members are vendor AutoML searches, not hand-built models;
    the SINGLE CatBoost 17th (the only one of the two SINGLE entries publishing scores; 47th publishes none)
    came within 0.00115 private AUC of the winning meta stack (0.98426 vs 0.98541).
- **New architectures at the board:** none. Every entrant used stock GBM/classical ML (CatBoost, XGBoost,
  LightGBM(+Dart), Random Forest, GaussianNB) or vendor AutoML wrappers; no `novel=` tag in any entry.
- **Agreed on (3 of 4 - S3E17-11, S3E17-17, S3E17-47):** CatBoost as a core model — 17th and 47th submit it
  solo; 11th keeps it as a level-1 member and notes it "performs exceptionally well" without external encoding.
  (S3E17-03 never names the estimators inside his AutoML stacks, so he does not count.)
- **Agreed on (2 of 4 - S3E17-17, S3E17-47):** merge the original train dataset with the playground train and
  drop exactly the `id`/`UID` columns; 17th also removes duplicates from the combined set (+0.002 attributed).
- **Agreed on (2 of 4 - S3E17-11, S3E17-17):** Optuna for hyperparameter selection (11th on the 4 GB members;
  17th on the single CatBoost, logged with Weights & Biases).
- **Agreed on (2 of 4 - S3E17-11, S3E17-17):** retrain the chosen model/ensemble on the FULL dataset for the
  final submission rather than shipping fold models (11th "retrained my ensemble on the whole dataset"; 17th
  "train on all data, and stop at the best iteration").
- **Divergences:** 3rd spent 6h of CPU on five blind AutoML searches + a meta layer and won with 0.98541
  private, publishing no features, no hyperparameters and no CV; 17th reached 0.98426 with one hand-tuned
  CatBoost and a 5-line feature list. The gap between those two architectures is 0.00115 AUC.
- CV regimes diverge with rank: 11th insists on 10-fold MultilabelStratifiedKFold inside a leakage-safe
  pipeline (rejects 5-fold as LB-uncorrelated); 17th iterated on a plain 80/20 hold-out and still beat most
  of the board.
- FE investment is inverted vs rank among the technical entries: 11th and 17th both report extra feature
  engineering as a wash; the only accepted FE across the set is keeping native categorical signals (product id
  as `cat_features`, +0.013 for 17th; CatBoostEncoder on non-CatBoost members for 11th).
- **Highest-leverage single trick:** pass product id (`pid`) to CatBoost via `cat_features` instead of dropping
  it with the other ids: +0.013 AUC (S3E17-17).
- **Nothing worked:** hand-engineered interaction/ratio features — 17th's `tratio`, `torque_mult_rspeed`,
  `torque_mult_toolwear` gave +0.004 public but ~0.000 private; 11th's extra FE attempts "did not improve".
- **Nothing worked:** GPU CatBoost (lower accuracy, non-deterministic, 17th) and 5-fold CV on this data
  (poor public-LB correlation, 11th).
