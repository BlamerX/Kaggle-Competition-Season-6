## S3E12 — consensus recipe
- **Architecture distribution:** 5th `FLAT` (soft vote of 4 stock models) · 8th `STACK2`
  (two vecstack LogReg-meta stacks over 9 bases, averaged) · 24th `FLAT` (3 GBMs × 8 folds,
  weight- and power-average-optimized) · 28th `FLAT` (8 families × 10 folds, optuna blend weights).
  winner topology: `FLAT` — 3 of 4 entries ranked on soft/weighted averaging with no meta-learner; the only stack finished 8th.
- **New architectures at the board:** none. All four entries use 2023 stock learners only
  (XGB/LGBM/CatBoost/RF/ET/GB/AdaBoost/LR/KNN/SVC); no entry submits a transformer or deep tabular model.
- **Agreed on (4 of 4 — S3E12-05, S3E12-08, S3E12-24, S3E12-28):** XGBoost in the model pool, and a
  K-fold CV scheme for tuning and/or selection (RSKfold for optuna, 10-fold, 8-fold, 10-fold respectively).
- **Agreed on (3 of 4 — S3E12-08, S3E12-24, S3E12-28):** CatBoost + LightGBM alongside XGBoost
  (S3E12-05 runs neither — his pool is RF/XGB/LR/KNN).
- **Agreed on (3 of 4 — S3E12-05, S3E12-08, S3E12-24):** distrust the public/private LBs and select on
  CV (05 cites the CV-0.841→182nd case; 08 "ignore the public LB"; 24 picked on CV "a good choice").
  S3E12-28 is the counter-example: he tuned 26 submissions against public LB and says his assumptions were wrong.
- **Probability calibration (the S3E12 discriminator):** only S3E12-24 does it —
  per-fold ROC-curve-based calibration fitted on validation + balanced/weighted target settings, then
  a power-average blend on top; per Ruling 2 the calibration is Post-processing, the weighting is
  weight selection (`FLAT`), and neither is a trained meta. S3E12-05 is a soft vote with weights not
  stated; S3E12-08 trains LogReg metas (the only true stacking); S3E12-28 selects blend weights by optuna.
- **Divergences:** top ranks kept it structurally simple (5th: 4-model soft vote; 24th: 3 GBMs),
  while 8th built the heaviest pipeline (2 stacks over 2 FE datasets) and 28th the widest pool
  (8 families, 26 submissions) — and neither published a score for its ranked submission (28th's
  0.77413 is a non-final experiment from the comments). No numeric gaps are computable: not
  one of the four states a public or private log-loss for the ranked submission.
- **Highest-leverage single trick:** IQR outlier treatment on training data (S3E12-28) — the only
  feature/data change that never hurt his LB score, and a quantile retune (0.3/0.7) plus dropping
  KNN/GradientBoosting would have moved 28th → 16th.
- **Nothing worked:** feature-engineering changes beyond the outlier treatment — 28th reports every FE
  attempt "always fell" on public LB; 24th tried "massive feature engineering" and reverted to
  scaling-only; 05 found the 4-model ensemble's CV *worse* than the standalone RF. Also submission
  volume as a search direction: 28th's 26 public-LB-chasing submissions ended with the better private
  variant left unsubmitted.
