  - Replace the training dataset's emission value by the new value of the COVID period.

## S3E20 — consensus recipe
- **Architecture distribution:** 3rd `SINGLE` (RF(100) inside BaggingRegressor(6)) · 4th `SINGLE` (XGBRegressor
  inside a PCA(6)/inverse-PCA wrapper).
  - **winner topology: `SINGLE`** — both medalists submitted one estimator family; no stack, blend or
    meta-learner appears anywhere in this competition's top-of-board record.
- **New architectures at the board:** none — every model named (RF, XGB, CatBoost, Ridge/Lasso, SARIMA) is
  stock; PCA-as-wrapper is a classical transform, not a novel architecture.
- **Agreed on (2 of 2 - S3E20-03, S3E20-04):** a constant-multiplier post-processing step on the final
  predictions, both credited to AMBROSM's discussion 428791 — 3rd shipped x1.07; 4th swept {0.95,1.00,1.05,
  1.07,1.08} and (self-reportedly) submitted the worst one.
- **Agreed on (1 of 2 - S3E20-04):** train the final model on ALL available data after model selection
  (4th: "Finally, all the data was used for training and prediction of the 2022 emissions"). The 3rd's page
  shows only `model.fit(X_train, y)` in the screenshot and never states what X_train holds — source silent.
- **Agreed on (2 of 2 - S3E20-03, S3E20-04):** the winning edge is data-centric, not architectural — 3rd
  converts COVID-period labels; 4th reorganizes the feature space with PCA; both then run one plain tree model.
- **Agreed on (2 of 2 - S3E20-03, S3E20-04):** a tree ensemble (RF / XGBRegressor) as the submitted estimator;
  4th additionally measured trees > linear (Ridge/Lasso).
- **Divergences:** the two medalists attack the same dataset from opposite ends: 3rd shrinks the problem
  (3 columns, row filter week_no<=48, repaired labels) while 4th expands it (all columns x 6 PCA components,
  n_columns*6 features). Both reach the podium with one XGB/RF-class model.
- CV discipline diverges: 4th built an explicit leave-one-year-out pipeline mirroring the temporal test split;
  3rd states no validation scheme at all — and neither publishes a CV number.
- **Highest-leverage single trick:** the x1.07 (or x1.00) constant multiplier from AMBROSM's post — it decided
  this podium in both directions: 3rd used 1.07 for bronze; 4th's own words: "It would be the 1st place, if I
  didn't use the multiplier at all!"
- **Nothing worked:** public-LB chasing — 4th's highest-public candidate (17.99) fell to 13.18 private (~230th)
  while his least-shakeuped model took 4th; XGB hyperparameter search ("not much difference", 4th); linear
  estimators (4th). 3rd states no failures at all.
