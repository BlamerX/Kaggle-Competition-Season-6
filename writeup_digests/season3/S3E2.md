### S3E2-05 · 5th · Jose Cáliz (jcaliz) · LB 0.89941 (only for the rejected 2nd submission; best not stated) · CV not stated numerically (10 StratifiedKFold OOF AUC)

- **Link:** https://www.kaggle.com/c/playground-series-s3e2/discussion/378780
- **Status:** FETCHED (jina `/c/` markdown form; 22-comment thread, incl. author replies, in the dump)
- **TL;DR:** Optimizer-weighted rank blend of 6 diverse models (2 LogisticRegression + 2 LightGBM + CatBoost + XGBoost); won on label-remap FE and a synthetic-only validation split.
- **Architecture:** FLAT · stages=1 · l1=6 models (2 LogisticRegression, 2 LightGBM, 1 CatBoost, 1 XGBoost) · l2=none trained meta — weights found by `scipy.optimize.minimize` (Nelder-Mead) over OOF, then predictions ranked · novel=none · mod=rank-blend ·
  topo=FE + concat(original) → 6 diverse models OOF → scipy/Nelder-Mead weights → rank predictions → submitted
  - Rule 1 applies: he searches blend weights with a Hessian-free optimizer (Nelder-Mead) on OOF predictions; selecting weights is not training a meta-learner, so `FLAT`, not `STACK2`.
  - The two `rank the predictions` steps are the blend mechanic; no LogReg/Ridge/NN fitted on an OOF matrix.
  - XGBoost (tuned, with FE) is "best single model"; the win came from the wide-model blend, not from one member.
- **Setup:** highly imbalanced binary target (`stroke`); concatenates the original (external) stroke dataset with the synthetic Playground data; "StratifiedKfold where validation only has synthetic data"; 10-fold stratified CV. Row/col counts not stated.
- **Features:** exact label remaps + engineered columns (author's `generate_features`):
  - Fill `smoking_status == 'unknown'` as `'never smoked'` (EDA shows `unknown` has the lowest stroke probability).
  - Fill `gender == 'other'` as `'male'` (spotted a CV boost; not validated on LB).
  - `age/bmi = age / bmi`; `age*bmi = age * bmi`; `bmi/prime = bmi / 25`; `obesity = avg_glucose_level * bmi / 1000`; `blood_heart = hypertension * heart_disease`.
  - Feature selection via Recursive Feature Elimination (RFE) is a named trick; the page states RFE explicitly only on the two LogisticRegression members, and LightGBM #1 runs "no RFE, no Feature Engineering"; whether LightGBM #2 (the logloss-monitored one) used RFE is not stated.
  - Encoding: OneHotEncoder is the kept choice; MeanEncoder / WoEEncoder / CountFrequencyEncoder tested and rejected (see Failed).
- **Models:** author-published membership (hyperparameters otherwise source silent):
  - LogisticRegression, RFE, L2, `liblinear` solver.
  - LogisticRegression, RFE, no regularization, `lbfgs` solver.
  - LightGBM, no RFE, no feature engineering.
  - LightGBM, early stopping, monitored on logloss ("logloss no AUC").
  - CatBoost (inspired by dmitryuarov's `ps-s3e2-catboost-lasso-nn-fiasco`), modified so OOF AUC ≈ mean per-fold AUC.
  - Tuned XGBoost + feature engineering — best single model; training code in his EDA notebook.
  - Seeds, exact grids, library versions: source silent.
- **CV:** 10 StratifiedKFold; trusts CV over LB because of "a good difference between by OOF AUC and the leaderboard" (page's own typo "by"); no numeric CV AUC published for the final blend.
  - CV-vs-LB gap: large by inspection (LB shake-up on the imbalanced, sample-count-sensitive AUC), which is why he ignored LB.
  - Trust verdict: "I decided to trust only my CV (10 StratifiedKfold)."
- **Ensembling:** weights from gradient/optimizer search on OOF predictions with `scipy.optimize.minimize`; ranks the blended predictions. Author comment: "AUC is not a convex function so any method that involves the Hessian may converge in few iterations. I used Nelder-mead in this competition." Shared-OOF usage: none (all members his own; community assets are FE/method inspiration, not prediction files).
- **Post-processing:** prediction ranking is part of the blend (rank-blend); no threshold/calibration/clip stated.
- **Gains:** (positive = AUC gain; magnitudes not published) gender `other`→`male` remap gave a "boost on CV"; RFE + label remaps + wide-model blend beat the XGBoost-only forward-selection submission. Exact deltas not quantified by the author.

  - MeanEncoder, WoEEncoder, CountFrequencyEncoder — none beat OneHotEncoder.

## S3E2 — consensus recipe
- **Architecture distribution:** 5th `FLAT` (scipy/Nelder-Mead weight search + rank blend of 6 diverse models) · 6th `FLAT` (Optuna weight search) · 8th `not stated` (notebook link only, no method).
  - **winner-of-file topology: `FLAT`** — the two detailed entries (5th, 6th) both blend level-0 models with optimizer-found weights; nobody fitted a meta-learner on OOF.
  - Both blends fall to `FLAT` by Tag Ruling 1: scipy/Nelder-Mead and Optuna weight *selection* is not `STACK2`.
- **New architectures at the board:** none — every named model (LogisticRegression, LightGBM, CatBoost, XGBoost) is a stock 2023 tabular learner. No novel architecture appears anywhere in the set.
- **Agreed on (2 of 3 — S3E2-05, S3E2-06): blend weights are found by an optimizer, not a trained meta-learner.** 5th uses `scipy.optimize.minimize` (Nelder-Mead, because AUC is non-convex); 6th uses Optuna. 8th publishes nothing.
- **Divergences:** 5th is a full FE + failure-log writeup (label remaps, RFE, engineered interactions, synthetic-only validation, 6-model blend); 6th reduces the whole solution to one blending trick; 8th reduces it to a single notebook link. The published AUC gap between them cannot be measured — only 5th quotes any LB number (0.89941, and for a submission he rejected).
- **Highest-leverage single trick:** (S3E2-05) remap degenerate categories to the low-risk class before modeling — `smoking_status unknown → never smoked` and `gender other → male` — then validate on synthetic-only folds so CV matches the LB shake-up.
- **Nothing worked:** (S3E2-05) MeanEncoder / WoEEncoder / CountFrequencyEncoder (all worse than OneHotEncoder), and a forward-selection XGBoost-only ensemble (private 0.89941, beaten by the wide-variety blend). No other entry reports dead ends.
