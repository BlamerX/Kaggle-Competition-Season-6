    three phases are mutually-exclusive routing (predict by lookup if a twin exists, else fit LR),
    so data does not flow forward through stages; one fitted model with a rule-based front-end
    stays `SINGLE`.
  - The architecture IS the validation choice: `GroupKFold(groups=train.squareMeters)` showed linear
    regression beats all tree-based models; plain `KFold` showed the duplicate-mean predictor wins.
    Which CV mirrors the LB is the whole finding.
- **Setup:** rows/cols not stated; final model uses only 3 features and the original dataset.
  - Structural quirk exploited: duplicated `squareMeters` values — test rows with a twin in training
    are lookups, not predictions (he announced this in "Quasi-duplicates in the data", 386116).

  duplicates + linear signal live in these three.
  - Original dataset included in the final model's data.
- **Models:** LinearRegression as phase-3 fallback only; tree models (stated as "all tree-based
  models") tested under GroupKFold and beaten; families/hyperparameters of that comparison not stated.
- **CV:** two schemes compared: `GroupKFold(groups=train.squareMeters)` and plain `KFold`; fold

## S3E6 — consensus recipe
- **Architecture distribution:** 3rd `STACK2` (CAT+XGB+RF → LGBM meta) · 5th `FLAT` (piece-wise
  made-splits, voting ensemble, no meta) · 8th `SINGLE` (HPO-tuned XGB; ensemble as 2nd slot) ·
  9th `AUTOML` (submitted the AutoGluon stack itself) · 43rd `SINGLE` (duplicate-mean lookups +
  linear fallback).
  - **winner topology: `STACK2`** (of the ranks covered here; the 1st/2nd writeups are not in the
    index) — but note the spread: everything from a trained meta (3rd) to almost no ML (43rd) fit
    inside 40 places, with no entry publishing an LB score to prove any of it.
- **New architectures at the board:** none — every submitted artifact is GBM/RF/LR/AutoGluon
  (S3E6-03, S3E6-05, S3E6-08, S3E6-09, S3E6-43); no TabPFN/transformer/MLP entry at any rank.
- **Agreed on (5 of 5 - S3E6-03, S3E6-05, S3E6-08, S3E6-09, S3E6-43):** include the **original
  (non-synthetic) dataset** in training — named explicitly by all five (9th excludes it from CV
  folds; 5th justifies it via adversarial validation on `squareMeters` alone; 43rd builds the
  lookups on it).
- **Agreed on (4 of 5 - S3E6-05, S3E6-08, S3E6-09, S3E6-43):** `squareMeters` is the load-bearing
  column — 5th's explicit dominance claim (~53% correlation, ~99% importance, "all the others were
  look to be noise"), 43rd keys the duplicate lookups on it, 8th's sentinel filter
  `squareMeters!=6071330` targets it, 9th stratifies CV by price-per-square-meter bins.
  S3E6-03 names no column at all.
- **Agreed on (2 of 5 - S3E6-05, S3E6-08):** explicit outlier removal before CV —
  IQR per column / 20 rows (5th) and the five sentinel-value filters on `made`, `floors`,
  `squareMeters`, `garage` (8th).
- **Agreed on (2 of 5 - S3E6-05, S3E6-08):** trust local CV over the public LB as a stated principle;
  8th quantified it (public rank ~400-500 → 8th, +409 places); 5th's 98%-trust cost him 3rd;
  a third-party version of the same lesson comes from Mellinger's comment on the 8th page (-300
  places for taking the LB over CV).
- **Agreed on (3 of 5 - S3E6-03, S3E6-05, S3E6-09):** repeated K-fold CV (5x10 for 3rd, multi-seed
  StratifiedKFold for 5th, 5-fold for 9th). 43rd (duplicate-aware GroupKFold) and 8th (binned
  per-fold CV) describe schemes too specific to fold into the count.
- **Agreed on (2 of 5 - S3E6-05, S3E6-09):** a **domain key as the stratifier**: `made` (5th),
  price/sqm bins (9th). S3E6-03 runs repeats but names no stratifier, so it is not in this count.
- **Divergences:** the top two ranks stacked/blended (3rd trained an LGBM meta; 5th a voting
  ensemble); the bottom of the set reached 43rd with **three columns and two lookup tables + linear
  regression**; 9th needed AutoGluon precisely because "I was not able to achieve high local
  cross-validation using a single model" (S3E6-09). The resulting score gap is unmeasurable — no
  entry publishes an LB RMSE; the only RMSE numbers in the set are 43rd's per-model CV bars
  (~183k-265k, chart image on S3E6-43).
- **Highest-leverage single trick:** treat duplicated `squareMeters` rows as lookups, not
  predictions — the mean price of exact (squareMeters, made, cityCode) twins (S3E6-43, announced
  first in discussion 386116; the soft version with KNN(5) even beat it, 39th per comment).
- **Nothing worked:** extra feature engineering beyond the raw columns (5th: "couldn't find features
