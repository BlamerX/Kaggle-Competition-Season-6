### S3E21-33 · 33rd · demko1 (Demyan Pavlyshenko) · LB not stated/not stated · CV 88-90% efficiency-retention figure only

- **TL;DR:** Cut to 3 features, hard-threshold the outliers, then distil an auxiliary XGB into the fixed
  RandomForest by using XGB's training predictions as RF's target — i.e., distillation used as target
  normalization / outlier handling.
- **Architecture:** CASCADE · stages=2 · l1=1 auxiliary XGBRegressor (not submitted) · l2=none (the fixed RF is the submitted model, retrained on XGB outputs) ·
  novel=none · mod=none ·
  topo=cleaned train -> XGB (3 cols + o2_mean) -> XGB TRAINING predictions as new target -> fixed RandomForest retrained (submitted)
  - Was the submitted model the fixed one? YES — he writes "distill XGB model into RandomForest (given in the
    competition)": the architecture the leaderboard scored is the provided RandomForest; the XGB is an
    auxiliary label generator that is never submitted.
  - `CASCADE`, not `PSEUDO`: XGB's predictions on the TRAINING rows become RF's targets in a single forward
    pass ("I first trained xgb model on its training data, and then used its training predictions as the
    training target for rf"); no test/unlabeled predictions are fed back, so there is no self-training loop
    (ruling 9 does not trigger; no `mod=pseudo` either).
  - `CASCADE`, not `STACK2`: nothing is blended — the earlier stage's output replaces the next stage's label,
    exactly the "output becomes input of the next stage" case; the author's own gloss: "distillation as a
    target normalizing, or, in other words, handling target outliers".
- **Setup:** water-quality regression under the competition's fixed-model format (page header: "Improve a
  Fixed Model the Data-Centric Way!"; the author writes the RandomForest was "given in the competition");
  the target column is not named on the page; train the fixed RF plus an auxiliary XGB; approach discussed
  in competition discussion 436434. Rows/cols/slots: not stated.

- XGB-only extra feature: `o2_mean` = overall mean of all o2 columns, including dropped ones (O2_3, O2_4, etc.).
- Outlier removal (both xgb and rf training data): drop all datapoints with `BSOD5_5` (author's spelling, i.e.

- XGB-only NaN masking: mark `O2_1, O2_2` values < 4 as NaN.
